## What has and has not been validated

- **FACT:** adjacent products span aerated coated bars, wafers, meringues, pastries, crisps, frozen desserts and dairy puddings; the linked official assortments support format/claim presence.
- **INFERENCE:** the closest strategic set is BUILT Puff + Flower & White + David + ProPud/Ehrmann: respectively aerated confection, meringue geometry, protein/calorie efficiency and dessert credibility.
- **UNKNOWN:** reliable revenues, funding, retail velocity, repeat rates and consumer complaint frequency for most private brands. These must not be fabricated in an investor or buyer deck.
- **ASSUMPTION TO KILL:** consumers will choose a dry, fragile meringue-like product over familiar bars/puddings at a premium. A blind taste test and priced preorder test are required.

## Review-mining protocol (next pass)

Export at least 100 recent verified reviews per closest product where terms permit; code only observable themes: aftertaste, sweetness, dryness, chew/crunch, coating melt, GI response, value, breakage, freshness and repeat intent. Report denominator and channel. Do not treat selected anecdotes as prevalence. Capture exact package images and nutrition labels in the data room with access date and market.
