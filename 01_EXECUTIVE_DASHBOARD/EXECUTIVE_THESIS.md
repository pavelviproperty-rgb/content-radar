# WEIGHTLESS: first-pass executive thesis

_Research cut: 9 August 2026. This is a decision memo, not legal, tax, food-science, or investment advice._

## Thesis in one page

**Recommendation — CONTINUE for 30 days, with a $10,000 kill-gated budget; do not raise or build a factory.** The smallest potentially transferable asset is not a scaled snack company. It is (1) a preferred, reproducible shelf-stable aerated dessert, (2) clean ownership and a viable mark, (3) quantified consumer willingness to pay, and (4) a manufacturer-ready technical package. Contract co-development in the United States or Canada is the baseline because speed, small pilots, simpler US compliance and low freight uncertainty matter more at validation scale than low foreign conversion cost.

The best first hypothesis is a **bite-size crisp aerated protein dessert**, not a 20–30 g rectangular bar. Bites reduce catastrophic breakage, let consumers understand “meringue/crisp,” permit portioning, and can reach a good experience before the extreme protein target. The first test should compare an egg/whey crisp against an egg/dairy blend and a softer aerated bite. Taste wins: use an 18 g serving if it clearly beats a 25 g version.

The economic risk is unusually important: a low-density product ships air, attracts dimensional freight, absorbs humidity, and breaks. A product is not commercially viable merely because ingredients are cheap. Month-one gates therefore include packaged drop testing, accelerated humidity exposure, actual co-manufacturer quotes, and fully loaded channel contribution—not a fictional ex-factory gross margin.

The likely fastest liquidity route is an **asset sale or exclusive license/JV** after technical and consumer proof, rather than a conventional VC round. A $1 million founder net exit probably requires materially more than a $1 million headline value after transaction costs and tax. Pre-revenue $1 million personal liquidity is possible but low-probability; no database found in this pass establishes it as a dependable outcome. Financing is company cash unless documents explicitly allocate a founder secondary.

### 30-day decision

* **SELL/LICENSE:** open conversations once prototype, rights, test results, and manufacturing package exist; accept only credible economics and diligence.
* **RAISE:** no institutional VC yet. Consider a strategic development advance only if it does not encumber all IP cheaply.
* **CONTINUE:** only if taste, durability, willingness-to-pay and quoted unit economics pass.
* **PIVOT:** to familiar bite/cookie/wafer or private-label platform if aerated crisp fails humidity or freight.
* **STOP:** if no prototype reaches preference parity, landed economics miss the gate, or clearance counsel finds the master brand unusable.

### Non-negotiable month-one gates

1. At least 60 target consumers; blinded preference versus two relevant products; ≥55% prefer the lead and lower 95% confidence bound is reported.
2. ≥40% top-two-box purchase intent at the tested price **and** ≥10% refundable-preorder conversion from qualified traffic (do not call a fake door “available”).
3. Post-packaging drop test: <5% unsaleable units; 14-day humidity screen preserves acceptable crispness.
4. Two written production quotations, including MOQ, lead time, packaging and quality certifications.
5. Fully loaded contribution margin ≥35% DTC before paid acquisition and ≥25% wholesale before overhead at the first credible run.
6. Written chain of title for formula, design and data; attorney knockout search before filing.
