# WEIGHTLESS — Top 30 investor targets (first-pass)

**Research date:** 2026-08-09  
**Purpose:** outreach prioritization for a capital-light, aerated high-protein dessert platform—not an assertion that any investor will fund the company or permit founder liquidity.

## How to read this list

- **Activity evidence** is a public portfolio, thesis, or program page that should be rechecked immediately before outreach. It does not prove a fund is currently deploying capital.
- **Stage/check** is stated only where the linked source makes it clear; otherwise it is **UNKNOWN—ask**. Never build the financing plan around an unsourced check-size directory.
- **Secondary alignment** is an inference. A fund investing in consumer brands is not evidence that it will buy founder shares or welcome a quick sale. Ask directly and obtain the answer in writing.
- **Fit score** is an internal 1–5 outreach-priority heuristic (5 = strongest apparent fit), not a probability of investment.
- **Primary capital is company money. Founder secondary is founder liquidity.** Keep both amounts separately identified in every term sheet and cap-table model.

## Required qualification questions (first call)

1. Is the fund actively making new investments, and what are its minimum ownership, initial check, and reserve targets?
2. Does it invest before commercial production, or what exact evidence is required?
3. Will it permit a strategic sale inside 12–36 months if the board concludes it is best?
4. Does it ever fund a **disclosed founder secondary**? At what revenue/round size, and what maximum percentage?
5. Can it introduce contract manufacturers, food scientists, retailers, and strategic acquirers?
6. Does it require US incorporation, domestic manufacturing, or geographic exclusivity?
7. Does it have conflicts through protein bar, meringue, confectionery, or dessert holdings?

## Data-quality / compliance note

This is a **lead list**, not diligence. Before any outreach, archive the investor's current team, portfolio, fund announcement, contact channel, and conflicts. Do not state or imply that “GLP-1 adjacent” products treat disease or cause weight loss. Never conceal an intended secondary or fast-exit preference; misalignment discovered after financing is expensive.
