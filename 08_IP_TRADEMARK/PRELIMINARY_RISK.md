# Preliminary trademark risk: WEIGHTLESS and WISP

_Desk screen only; not an attorney clearance opinion._

| Mark | Preliminary risk | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| WEIGHTLESS | **Medium–high / UNKNOWN** | Reserve domains/handles; do not commit packaging; commission knockout search now | Ordinary English word with suggestive/descriptive weight and lightness meanings; broad common-law and registry searching across food, supplements, retail and adjacent wellness is necessary. |
| WISP | **High** | Treat as provisional and develop replacements | Short dictionary term in a crowded naming field; proximity to Mondelēz/Cadbury **WISPA** chocolate creates avoidable confusion/opposition risk even if exact goods/classes differ. |

## Required clearance sequence

1. Search exact, phonetic, plural and design variants in USPTO, WIPO Global Brand Database, EUIPO and UKIPO, including dead records and owners' related marks.
2. Search common-law use across Google, Amazon, retailers, app stores, domains and social handles.
3. Have counsel assess Classes 5, 29, 30, 32 and 35 and the actual goods wording; clearance is based on confusing similarity, not exact-match availability.
4. File only after the lead product and intended markets are chosen. Obtain assignments from every designer and developer.

Official starting points: [USPTO search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search), [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/), [EUIPO eSearch](https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/), [UKIPO trademark search](https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmtext), and [Cadbury Wispa product page](https://www.cadbury.co.uk/products/cadbury-wispa-chocolate-bar-36g/).

**Conclusion:** neither mark is cleared. WEIGHTLESS is worth a counsel-led knockout search; WISP should not be treated as a bankable asset unless counsel clears it, and replacement naming should run in parallel.
