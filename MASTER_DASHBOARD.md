## Founder-money discipline

| Cash type | Founder liquidity? |
|---|---|
| Primary investment | No; company cash |
| Founder secondary | Yes, gross sale proceeds |
| Acquisition cash | Yes, subject to ownership, preferences, holdbacks and tax |
| Licensing fee/royalty | Company income unless paid/ distributed; tax varies |
| Salary/consulting | Personal taxable compensation, not sale proceeds |
| Earn-out | Contingent; discount heavily for timing/performance risk |
| Rollover equity | Illiquid until a later event |

Professional tax and transaction advice is required before relying on any “net” outcome.
