# WEIGHTLESS capital scenarios: buy evidence, not infrastructure

**Status:** first-pass operating model (2026-08-09)  
**Decision rule:** Release each tranche only after the prior tranche passes its kill gates. All dollars are **company spend**, not founder liquidity. Estimates exclude founder salary, tax, and a factory.

## Common assumptions

- **ASSUMPTION:** Founder starts with a reproducible kitchen prototype and can contribute project management without salary for 30 days.
- **ASSUMPTION:** Launch route is a US entity, external food scientist/pilot facility, and a shelf-stable product sold initially in the US. Offshore commercial production is not funded until landed economics and import compliance are quoted.
- **FACT:** FDA says a private residence is not a facility required to register, but food offered for US consumption is subject to applicable federal, state and local requirements; commercial facilities that must register do so with FDA. FDA does not charge a registration fee. [FDA, *How to Start a Food Business*](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-industry/how-start-food-business); [FDA, *Registration of Food Facilities and Other Submissions*](https://www.fda.gov/food/registration-food-facilities-and-other-submissions/registration-food-facilities-and-other-submissions) (accessed 2026-08-09).
- **FACT:** USPTO's base application fee is $350 per class, with additional fees possible for custom identifications or deficient/incomplete applications. The budgets reserve more because search and counsel—not the government fee—are the costly risk controls. [USPTO trademark fee information](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/fees-and-payment/trademark-fee-information) (accessed 2026-08-09).
- **ESTIMATE:** Costs below are planning allowances, not vendor quotes. A minimum of three written quotes is a gate before committing more than $2,500 to any workstream.

## Spend gates

1. **Release $0–5k:** only on taste/formulation, ownership documentation, trademark screen and quote gathering.
2. **Release $5–10k** only if ≥60% of at least 30 target consumers prefer the prototype to a named benchmark **and** median stated WTP supports a channel-feasible price.
3. **Release $10–25k** only with two manufacturers confirming process plausibility and indicative MOQ, plus a model showing ≥40% delivered DTC contribution before paid acquisition or ≥35% brand gross margin on wholesale at realistic landed COGS.
4. **Release $25–50k** only after pilot sensory acceptance, packaging breakage test, safety plan, and no fatal trademark issue.
5. **Release $50–100k** only if preorder/small-run evidence produces either (a) ≥100 non-friend purchasers with ≥20% 60-day reorder intent/behavior, or (b) a signed strategic/JV/retailer-funded milestone agreement.

## Capital-source order (least dilution first)

1. Founder funds only the $5k falsification sprint.
2. Manufacturer-subsidized development, paid pilot credit, grants, preorder or crowdfunding **after** a safe/compliant production route exists.
3. Strategic licensing option/JV advance tied to milestones.
4. Small aligned angel SAFE only when it purchases a discrete value inflection.
5. Institutional equity only when a strategic transaction is not yet available and data supports a much larger exit.

These fees exclude fulfillment, returns and cost of goods. [Kickstarter fees](https://help.kickstarter.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005028634-What-are-the-fees) (accessed 2026-08-09).

## Founder-liquidity guardrails

- A **$500k SAFE wired to WEIGHTLESS is $0 founder liquidity** unless documents expressly authorize a secondary purchase.
- Salary and consulting fees are compensation, not sale proceeds; earn-outs are contingent, not cash at close; rollover equity is illiquid.
- Do not permit secondary proceeds from scarce seed capital unless founder receives enough to matter and company retains enough runway to meet transaction milestones.
- Avoid stacking preferences. A 1x non-participating preference can still divert sale proceeds ahead of common; model every exit waterfall before accepting capital.
