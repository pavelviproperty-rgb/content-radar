## Coverage gaps and outreach rule

The initial list deliberately includes **commercial factories plus pilot/R&D partners** because public directories rarely disclose minimums or available lines. It has credible leads in the US, Canada, UK, Netherlands, Germany, Poland, Czech Republic, Mexico, Australia, New Zealand and Spain. **UNKNOWN/under-covered:** France/Belgium/Italy meringue specialists, Baltics, Ireland, Brazil/Argentina/Chile, Turkey, Japan/Korea/Taiwan/Singapore, China and India. Do not send formula detail to any lead before mutual NDA, background-IP schedule, foreground-IP clause, non-use language, and confirmation that WEIGHTLESS owns transferable batch records/SOPs.

## One-page RFQ fields (send to all 20)

Ask the same questions to make scores comparable: smallest paid bench/pilot/commercial run; equipment (whip/aeration, depositing, low-temperature bake/dry, extrusion, freeze dry, coating, flow-wrap, nitrogen flush, tray/carton); protein/egg experience; target water activity; allergen segregation; GFSI scheme and certificate; US/EU export history; ingredient and packaging procurement; development/line-trial fees; finished unit estimate at 1k/5k/25k/100k; lead time; scrap/breakage; Incoterm; shelf-life support; change-control; recall/insurance; formula/process ownership; non-compete/exclusivity; and two customer references.
