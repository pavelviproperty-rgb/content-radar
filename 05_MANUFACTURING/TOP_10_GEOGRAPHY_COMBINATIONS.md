## Recommendations

- **OPTION 1: US development / US manufacture / Delaware / US test.** Start here unless comparable quotations show a ≥15% landed-COGS advantage abroad at the same cash MOQ, lead time and quality. The aim is not patriotism; it is eliminating an import workstream during the 30-day validation sprint.
- **OPTION 2: US development / Canadian manufacture / US sales.** Run concurrently, especially with Ontario/Quebec bar/bakery manufacturers. Do not assume tariff-free treatment or USMCA origin without a broker’s ingredient-level review.
- **OPTION 3: UK development and manufacture / UK test.** Best independent route if an existing meringue line accepts a pilot and US factories require custom high MOQs.

## Explicitly not top-three yet

China and India can offer scale but are not justified for the first fragile, formulation-sensitive run: supplier qualification, IP controls, ocean/air freight and US-import compliance can erase conversion savings. Turkey, Mexico, Poland and Brazil remain RFQ candidates—not decisions. Country-level labor cost is never a substitute for a factory-level landed quote.
