# WEIGHTLESS — Top 30 strategic buyers / licensees (first-pass)

**Research date:** 2026-08-09  
**Status:** hypothesis-driven target database. A public portfolio or acquisition history demonstrates strategic adjacency, **not** acquisition intent.

## Scoring and stages

- **Early** = potentially interested in formulation/IP, option, license, co-development, or a small proof package.
- **Traction** = likely requires repeat, velocity, margin, shelf-life, and scalable co-manufacturing evidence.
- **Scale** = more plausible after material revenue/distribution.
- **Fit score** (1–5) is outreach priority, not an estimated deal probability.
- Corporate structures change. Confirm the legal buyer, decision maker, conflicts, and current portfolio before disclosure; use staged non-confidential outreach before sharing trade secrets.

## Outreach tiers

### Tier 1 — approach after a credible pilot and consumer proof
Mondelēz, Mars, Ferrero, Hershey, Simply Good Foods, BellRing, Glanbia, Nestlé. Send a **non-confidential** 1-page teaser first. Minimum package: blind sensory results, verified nutrition, 90-day accelerated/real-time shelf-life trend, pilot yields, landed COGS, breakage test, purchase-intent and paid-order evidence, clean IP chain of title.

### Tier 2 — channel/product-route dependent
General Mills, Danone, Unilever, Grupo Bimbo, pladis, Valeo, THG/Myprotein, Nutrabolt. Match the pitch to the format: frozen whip to dairy/ice cream groups; dry crisp/wafer to bakery/snack groups; white-label launch to nutrition platforms.

### Tier 3 — technology license / co-development
Arla Foods Ingredients, FrieslandCampina Ingredients, dsm-firmenich, Döhler, and potentially Glanbia. Do not disclose exact ratios/process parameters until an NDA and foreground/background IP language have been reviewed. A paid development agreement must state who owns improvements and commercialization data.

## Evidence gates by transaction

| Transaction | Minimum credible evidence package | Founder-liquidity reality |
|---|---|---|
| Paid option / evaluation | Transferable formula file, samples, basic safety/nutrition, defined exclusivity field and option deadline | Usually modest option fee; not automatically $1M |
| Regional/category license | Repeatable pilot, shelf life, claims review, COGS, trademark rights, license economics, QA tech transfer | Upfront + minimum guarantee + royalty can create liquidity, but tax/ownership treatment varies |
| Brand/IP asset sale | Clean trademark/formula ownership, consumer proof, packaging, manufacturer handoff, no undisclosed claims/liabilities | Acquisition proceeds; transaction costs and taxes reduce founder net |
| Majority/acquisition | Above plus clean corporate records, cap table, contracts, insurance, financials, channel data, scalable supply | Founder cash, earn-out, rollover, and escrow must be shown separately |
| Strategic investment + secondary | Financing milestone plan, valuation support, board alignment, disclosed allocation of primary vs secondary | Only the secondary portion is immediate founder liquidity |

## Buyer-screen questions

1. Is the interest in **brand**, **technology**, **manufacturing know-how**, **regional rights**, or **private label**?
2. What proof gate and internal budget owner are required for an evaluation?
3. What exclusivity is requested by product, channel, territory, and duration?
4. Who owns co-developed improvements, stability data, tooling, and failed trial learnings?
5. Will consideration be cash at close, earn-out, royalty, consulting, employment, or rollover equity? Model each separately.
6. Are there minimum guarantees, development reimbursement, diligence cost shifting, indemnities, escrow, or non-compete restrictions?

## Critical cautions

- An ingredient supplier offering free formulation support may require its ingredient, minimum volumes, publicity rights, or ownership of improvements; this can reduce transferability.
- Retail exclusivity can validate demand but may deter a broader buyer if term, territory, and channel are not narrow.
- Do not promise “weight management,” “GLP-1,” “low calorie,” “high protein,” or other regulated claims until substantiated for the target jurisdiction.
- Verify the current owner and legal entity for every target immediately before outreach, particularly groups undergoing announced acquisitions or reorganizations.
