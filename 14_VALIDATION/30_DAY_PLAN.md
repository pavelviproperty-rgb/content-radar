# Thirty-day build-to-exit sprint

| Days | Product | Manufacturing / regulatory | Market / deal | Deliverable / gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Write target product profile; document baseline formula | Send structured RFI to 20 leads | Counsel trademark knockout; recruit panel | Signed IP assignments; test protocol |
| 4–7 | Food scientist makes 3 systems × 2 formats | Screen allergen, aw, process and packaging risks | Price/concept test without efficacy claims | Select 3 prototypes, not one |
| 8–12 | Instrument weights, yield, breakage, aw | Calls with ≥8 partners; obtain pilot/MOQ details | Blind central-location test, n≥60 | Taste and price gate |
| 13–16 | Iterate lead; begin humidity and drop screens | Shortlist 5; request written quotes and NDA/ownership terms | Launch transparent prelaunch page | Formula v1 and qualified traffic baseline |
| 17–21 | Package samples; calculate nutrition provisionally | Compare US/Canada/Mexico/EU landed scenarios | Creator/sample test and 10 buyer discovery calls | Two credible quotes; objections log |
| 22–26 | Confirm repeat batch by independent operator | Draft product spec, COA/testing and recall requirements | Collect refundable preorders; investor discovery only | Reproducibility and conversion gates |
| 27–30 | Freeze or kill v1 | Select pilot route; regulatory counsel check | Build buyer teaser/data room; decision meeting | CONTINUE, LICENSE, PIVOT or STOP memo |

## Day-30 answers required

* Technical viability: replicated formula, water activity and failure observations.
* First format: selected by taste, durability and economics—not founder preference.
* Manufacturing: five verified candidates, two quotes, one backup process.
* Market: tested price and channel with observed behavior.
* Capital: bottom-up pilot cash requirement and timing.
* Transferability: chain of title, formula/SOP, test data, supplier/manufacturer record.

## Outreach package

Use one RFI with: target format (not secret formula), allergens, nutrition targets, process hypotheses, package, annual ranges, pilot size, target markets, certifications, desired launch date, R&D ownership, exclusivity, MOQ, lead time and delivered quotation. Do not disclose enabling know-how before NDA and ownership terms are reviewed.
